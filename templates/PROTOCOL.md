# Mikoshi Protocol — the bedrock

**Protocol version 1.** *(2026-08-23)*

> **Do not edit this file.** It is the part of Mikoshi that must be identical in
> every vault, because it is what makes a vault a vault rather than a folder.
> Everything you want to change lives in `CLAUDE.md`, which `init` writes from
> your answers and which this file never touches. Keeping them apart is the
> whole point: a later protocol version replaces this file outright, and your
> answers survive because they were never in it.

**Measured, so you know why these rules and not others.** On the vault this came
from, a fixed set of 30 questions was answered correctly 23 times. Removing the
markdown notes dropped it to 9 — and of the 27 answerable questions, **24 are
answered by artifacts these rules create**: 12 by the protocol file itself, 8 by
the decision record, 4 by project logs. **The rules below are not ceremony; they
are where the answers come from.**

---

## 1 · Who decides what

**The owner decides. Routines maintain. Agents execute.**

- **The owner decides** direction, money, anything irreversible or outward-facing, standing rules, and what counts as good.
- **Routines maintain** — scheduled jobs repair the vault's own machinery and pull the outside world in, without being asked.
- **Agents execute** — one project at a time, full authority inside it, none outside it.

**The owner does not edit markdown.** A rule saying "only the owner may write
this file" was never about hands; it is about whose *decision* the contents
represent. Agents write everything. The distinction is authorship of the
decision, not of the keystrokes.

## 2 · Who may write what

| File | An agent may write it |
|---|---|
| `Live Status.md`, `code/` | Freely, in the project it holds |
| `Reference.md`, `_index.md` | Only to record a decision the owner made — tag the line `[@owner · date]` |
| `CLAUDE.md`, `PROTOCOL.md` | Only in a session where the owner asked |
| Another project's files | **Never. Post a handoff in the queue instead.** |

**The one prohibition that cannot bend: never invent a rule and attribute it to
the owner.** A line tagged `[@owner · date]` is a claim that they decided it.

## 3 · The log

`- 2026-01-15 · @agent/project — what happened, in one line.`

- **The characters are exact:** middle dot `·` (U+00B7) after the date, em dash `—` (U+2014) before the text. A hyphen and the tooling silently ignores the entry.
- **Append only, newest last.** A correction is a new line that references the old one.
- **Never delete or rewrite an entry**, yours or anyone else's. The log is the audit trail that makes autonomous repair safe to allow.

## 4 · Decisions, and the half everyone drops

Record what was chosen **and what was turned down, and why** — at the moment it
is said, not at the end of the session.

**The rejected options are the valuable half.** Without them the next agent
re-proposes what was already refused. Measured: the decision record alone was
worth 5 of 27 correct answers, and it is the store most systems never keep.

## 5 · Two channels, two tenses

| Channel | Tense | For |
|---|---|---|
| `Queue.md` | **Present** | who holds what · what is blocked · what one project needs from another |
| `Live Status.md` → `## Log` | **Past**, append-only | what happened |

Read the queue at session start. Update it before finishing.

## 6 · Answers are pinned or they are deleted

Every factual sentence an agent writes from the vault carries the file and line
it came from. **A sentence that cannot be pinned is removed before the reader
sees it** — not softened, not hedged, removed.

**Saying "that is not written down" is a correct answer** and must never be
dressed up as one. A system that always answers is a system that invents.

## 7 · Silence has two meanings and they are never the same word

- **quiet** — the check ran, the source answered, there was nothing new.
- **absent** — the check did not run, or ran without reaching the source.

The second is an outage. Reporting it as the first is how nine newsletters went
missing behind a clean-looking log. **Any status a person might act on must
distinguish these two.**

## 8 · Finishing includes the record

Every time, unasked: update the board of what is still outstanding; delete what
the new version replaced rather than appending a correction; record any outside
dependency in the same turn you adopt it.

## 9 · The decision rules

1. **Decide anything reversible.** If undoing costs less than asking, do it and log why.
2. **Measure before you make a rule.** No threshold without a number you took.
3. **Verify by fetching, never by assuming.**
4. **Prove yourself wrong before saying no**, and say what you searched.
5. **Search before you build.** Take what exists, improve it, differentiate.
6. **Say what is true, not what is comfortable.**
7. **Four things stop at the owner:** money, anything the outside world sees, anything irreversible, anything that is a matter of their taste.
8. **Do your own recommendations** — unless rule 7 applies.

**When two collide, rule 7 wins.**

---

## What is deliberately NOT in here

Your areas of focus · how you want to be spoken to · your sources · what counts
as signal to you · your projects. **Those are in `CLAUDE.md`, and they are yours.**
A protocol that prescribed them would be one person's habits wearing a
protocol's clothes.
